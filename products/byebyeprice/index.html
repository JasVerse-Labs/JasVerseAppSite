ByeByePrice

BETA

What it is

An anti-scam price comparison engine: it pulls real listings from real sellers (eBay and affiliate networks) and surfaces an honesty signal on top of the raw price, instead of just the lowest number.

Problem

Price-comparison sites are easy to game with manipulated "was" prices, hidden shipping costs, and fake discounts. Most comparison tools show whichever price is lowest without checking whether it's honest.

Current reality

Live and in active use at byebyeprice.com. It is real, working software with a production deployment, a daily price-snapshot pipeline and a real database of price history — not a mockup. It is still labeled BETA because it is actively evolving and has known open gaps (for example, data-freshness depends on a scheduled ingestion job that can lag).

Capabilities

Real-time product search against live marketplace data · a "SafePrice" honesty score computed from actual price history (never fabricated when there isn't enough history to support it) · real-cost calculation including shipping · installable as a Progressive Web App on mobile.

Current distribution surfaces

What exists now

A deployed web application and PWA, a production database of historical prices, and an automated ingestion pipeline.

What does not exist yet

No native Android or iOS app (no app-store listing of any kind). No verified public API for third parties.

Current next step

Continue hardening the existing web/PWA product before considering a native mobile wrapper (see Products for the ecosystem's web-first distribution approach).
